How to play

Touch anywhere on the road and slide your finger left or right. The bus follows your finger.

Steer with ← → or A / D.

Each pothole you hit costs a tyre. Grab the yellow + token to fix one. The bus keeps getting faster.

Tap the pause button in the top corner to stop, change settings or quit.
